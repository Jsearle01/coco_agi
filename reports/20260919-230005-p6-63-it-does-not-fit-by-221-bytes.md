## 7A — ★★★★★ RULING REQUESTED — 221 bytes

**Text and sprites cannot be in one binary today. The gap is 221 bytes, after taking the one free
move.** Everything the integrated run needs is behind this — the copyright, the title scroll, and
any screen that shows a message box while a sprite is on it.

**What is already free, and I recommend taking regardless of what you decide:**

> **Relocate `vm_tables.s` out of region A in the cel arm — 626 bytes.** The text arms have done
> this for tasks; the `org`, the assertion and the host-side handling all exist. It is a two-line
> conditional change and it helps the cel arm whether or not text ever joins it.

**The remaining 221 bytes need one of three things, and the choice is yours:**

1. ★★★★ **Move `CP_CEL` above `$6000`** — 256 B, and it closes the gap with 35 to spare. It is a
   **256-byte row buffer**, not the 4,784-byte staging buffer it used to be, so this is a much
   smaller question than when it was last asked. **§6 of this dispatch declared it closed, which is
   why I am asking rather than doing.**
2. ★★★ **Shrink 221 bytes of region A code.** Real but unbudgeted, and this arc has added to region
   A in six of the last eight tasks.
3. ★★ **A third region.** The largest change and the one I would not take for 221 bytes.

**What I would not do:** split the difference by putting *part* of the text engine in. The nine
handlers are what `display` needs and they are not separable from the engine that renders glyphs.

**One thing found on the way that is not about space at all**, and the next task needs it whichever
way you rule: **a combined arm has two consumers of one keystroke.** The direction poll and the text
editor both call `HAL_key_scan`, which reports matrix state and has no key-up. The oracle settles it
— `handleController` consumes direction keys and returns `true`, and only a key it did *not* consume
reaches the text input — so this is a mirroring job, not a design problem, but it is not free and it
is not in anyone's estimate yet.
