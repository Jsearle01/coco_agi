### 1 — Summary

★★★★★ **THE HEADLINE IS A REFUTATION, AND IT IS MINE.** Two titles suggested the VM's cost was
`fixed + k × opcount`, with ~59,558 cycles of fixed overhead — a tidy answer to the unattributed
36.6%. **Four more titles destroyed it: R² = 0.018. Opcode count explains 2% of the variance.**

| title | cy/VM cycle | opcount | cy/opcode | cyc/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KQ3 | 191,690 | 361 | 531 | 9.3 |
| SQ1 | 134,332 | 251 | 535 | 13.3 |
| KQ1 | 126,902 | 184 | 690 | 14.1 |
| larry1 | 118,283 | 355 | 333 | 15.1 |
| BlackCauldron | 113,825 | 343 | 332 | 15.7 |
| PQ1 | 106,395 | 343 | 310 | 16.8 |

★★★★ **larry1 and KQ3 run effectively the same opcount (355 vs 361) for 62% different cost.** The
cheapest cycle in the corpus (PQ1) is also one of the busiest. **Whatever dominates the VM's cycle
is not the number of opcodes it runs**, which forecloses the most natural optimisation target and
is worth more than the wrong number it replaces.

★★★★ **AC-3's ablation is reported WITH ITS CHECK BROKEN, not without.** `opcount` — the
work-invariance check this project has leaned on since AD-87 — **is constant across every arm,
including one that should zero it.** The free-run loop skips interpretation, then the handshake
path's *unguarded* `jsr vm_interpret_cycle` runs one cycle at the end; that cycle is `logic.0`'s
initialisation and carries nearly all 184 commands (KQ1 averages 0.9/cycle). **So opcount is
dominated by one init cycle every arm runs once, and cannot distinguish "faster" from "not doing
the work" in free-run mode.**

★★ **AC-2 reconciles**: 6,717 of 6,772 VM bytes symbol-attributed, **55 unattributed (0.8%)**.

### 3 — Reasoning

**★★★★★ Why the two-point model was worth publishing and then killing.** Two points define a line
exactly: there is no residual, nothing in the fit that *could* have contradicted it. I quoted
59,558 cycles of fixed overhead from KQ1 and KQ3 and said so mid-task; four more titles cost one
background run and turned a confident wrong number into a real constraint. ★★★ **L-41's shape
exactly — a ratio is not a cost, and a two-point fit is a ratio with ceremony.**

**★★★★ What the six titles DO constrain.** Cost/cycle spreads 1.80×; cost/opcode spreads 2.22×;
the two are uncorrelated. So the driver is per-title and not script length — object count in
`vm_post_cycle`'s motion, resource fetches per cycle, and room size are the candidates the data
admits. ★★ **Named, not ranked** (§2): this data cannot order them.

**★★★★★ Why the ablation ladder's numbers are quoted as unverified.** L-73 asks what else each
toggle moves, and the honest answer here is *I cannot tell*, because the instrument that would
tell me is broken in this mode:

| arm | cy/VM cycle | opcount | implied |
|---|---|---|---|
| baseline | 126,902 | 184 | — |
| `VM_NOCOUNT` | 122,592 | 184 | instrumentation **4,310 = 3.40%** |
| `VM_PACEONLY` | 17,386 | 184 | harness floor **13.7%**; interpret+post **86.3%** |
| `ABL_NOCOPY` | 40,418 | 184 | resource copy **68.2%** |

★★★ **`VM_NOCOUNT` is the one arm I trust**: it removes counters only, cannot change control flow,
and 3.40% is a bounded, plausible cost. ★★★★ **The other two are not trustworthy on this
evidence.** `ABL_NOCOPY` skips materialising resources, so later logic may take different paths —
and opcount, which exists to detect exactly that, is blind here. **68.2% is an upper bound, not a
measurement.**

**★★★ The dispatch's own priors move.** It carries "the harness floor at 5.77% and instrumentation
at 1.44%"; measured here they are **13.7% and 3.40%** — both roughly 2.4× larger. ★ Stated as a
discrepancy to resolve, not as a correction: those figures may have been taken on a different
title, build or clock, and **this task did not establish which.**

**Authority tier.** Measurement of our own tree; no ScummVM or Specs claim. **§2S:** POP `104b197`
