### 3 — Reasoning

**§3(2) — the five labels, instruction by instruction, with addressing modes.** All of
`composite.s`'s variables are **extended**: `lda >ext` 5 · `ldx >ext` 6 · `stx >ext` 6 · `sta >ext` 5
· `cmpa >ext` 5 · `inc >ext` 7 · `dec >ext` 7 · `lda ,x+` 6 · `bne` 3 · `lbra`/`lbeq` 5.

```
co_pix   lda co_remw 5 · lbeq co_rownext 5 · ldx co_src 6 · lda ,x+ 6 · stx co_src 6
         sta co_col 5 · cmpa co_key 5 · bne co_opaque 3          = 41 opaque
         + bra co_nextx 3 on the transparent fall-through        = 44 transparent
co_nextx inc co_curx 7 · dec co_remw 7 · lbra co_pix 5           = 19
```

**§3(3) — the direct page, and it is not ours.** ★★★★★ `setdp 0` appears in `file.s`, `gfx.s`,
`hal_globals.s`, `input.s`, `irq_vbl.s`, `mem.s` — **page 0 is the HAL's**, and it is occupied:
`hal_frame_hi` at DP `$10/$11`, `blit_tmp`. ★★★ `composite.s` is `org`ed into region A at `$5xxx`, so
**every access to `co_src`, `co_remw`, `co_col`, `co_key`, `co_curx`, `co_rowpri` pays the extended
penalty** — one cycle and one byte per access. ★★★★ **Moving them into page 0 needs `MAP_STATUS`
space and would change every HAL `<` access**: §6's fifth trigger, **priced and not built** (§4B(2)).

**§3(4) — what holds X, Y and U, and what each plane call clobbers.**

| register | holder | across the pixel loop? |
|---|---|---|
| X | `plane_vis`/`plane_pri` **return** an address in it; `co_st_put` writes through it | ★★★★★ **not available** |
| U | `co_put_visual` and `co_inc32` (`ldu #co_tested`) | ★★★ **not available** |
| **Y** | ★★★★★ **nothing in the live build** | ★★★★★ **free** |

★★★★★ **Y's freedom is checked, not assumed**: the only `y` in `composite.s` is at lines 889–921, in
`co_sv_row`/`co_sv_px`/`co_sv_rest`, inside `ifdef CP_SAVE` — which **P6.56 established has never
been assembled**. `plane_win.s` works in A, B, D and returns X; `co_inc32` saves A, B and X.
★★ `vc_decode_row` is called **per row**, before Y is loaded, so it cannot matter.

★★★★ **`co_col`'s only reader is `co_put_visual`** (three reads), which is on the **drawn** path —
that is what makes §4B(1) safe. ★★★ **`co_src` is written at three sites** (`cp_composite` once per
cel, `co_row`'s cache-hit branch, and after `vc_decode_row`) and read only by `co_pix`.

★★★★★ **The trap in §4B(3), and it is why `sty` exists**: in the **non**-`COMP_ROW_PULL` build
(`comp_probe`) `co_src` is set **once per cel** and advances continuously across rows via `co_pix`'s
`stx`. With the per-pixel store gone, **only `sty co_src` at `co_rownext` carries the position from
one row to the next.** Without it the flat build would restart every row at the same source byte.

**Authority tier.** All measured in this tree at a measured clock (1.789772 MHz). ★ The instruction
cycle counts are **my own arithmetic from the 6809's addressing modes, labelled unverified per §8** —
but §4A's whole purpose was to check them against the sampler, and they agree to within 3%.

**§2H's three checks.** (1) *A second mechanism for a different object class?* ★★★★★ **Yes, and it
decided the implementation**: the flat build (`comp_probe`) uses `co_src` differently from the
row-pull build — continuously across rows rather than reset per row — so the fix had to preserve
`co_src` as the home of the fact rather than replace it. (2) *The calling routine.* `co_pix` is
entered from `co_nextx`'s `lbra` 725 times a cycle; its cost is per **tested** pixel, while
`co_opaque`'s is per **opaque** one — which is why `sta co_col`'s saving is weighted by the 55%
transparent share, not by the pixel count. (3) *Grep the reports.* P6.99 (the decomposition and its
zero-length caveat), P6.98 (per-call overhead, and predict-then-measure), P6.56 (`CP_SAVE` never
assembled), P6.84 (measure separately) all agree.

**§2S.** No sibling claim is made.
