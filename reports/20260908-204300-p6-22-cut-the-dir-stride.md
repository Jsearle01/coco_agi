### 1 — Summary
**A landed, and the dispatch's number was wrong for a reason worth keeping.** The map's comment
said "341 slots vs 216 present max"; **216 is the count of PRESENT resources, and what a directly
indexed table must hold is the highest INDEX.** Across **9 Sierra and 147 fan titles** that index
is **255 in 66 of them, PoliceQuest1 included** — so 704 would have truncated a gate title. The
correct cut is **768, and it is structural rather than statistical**: an AGI resource number is a
byte, so 256 × 3 is the format's ceiling and no title can ever exceed it. That frees **1,024
bytes**, and `get.string` now fits with **620 spare** while `MAP_RESERVED` returns to **320 free
for sound**. The key decoder is built — **318 bytes, in `hal_globals.s`, which is PROJECT_LOCAL, so
POP and Karateka are not merely byte-identical: their inputs never changed.** ★★★ **The stride
constant turned out to have FIVE homes**, and finding the fifth cost the VM gate 0/9 across every
title. **AC-8 through AC-10 and AC-1 are not reached** and §6 says exactly what is missing.

### 3 — Reasoning

**A. The comment measured the wrong thing, and the census says so.** [authority: measurement]
A DIR is addressed directly — entry N at offset 3N — so the table must span the highest **index**,
not the count of present entries. `dir_census.py` over **9 Sierra + 147 fan = 156 v2 titles**
(9 skipped as not-v2):

| type | highest slot | present | title |
|---|---|---|---|
| logdir | 256 | 17 | aquest (fan) |
| picdir | 256 | 33 | bormn1 (fan) |
| viewdir | 256 | 220 | **PoliceQuest1 (Sierra)** |
| snddir | 256 | 3 | dateq1 (fan) |

★★★ **I verified the suspicious ones by reading the bytes** rather than trusting the count:
PoliceQuest1's viewdir carries a real entry `33d0ed` at index 255, and aquest's logdir `005dff` at
255 with only 17 present. **Sparse tables with a live entry at the top are normal, and that is
exactly the case a "present max" hides.**

**B. 768 is the format's ceiling, so this is not AD-143's hazard.** [authority: the AGI format]
An AGI resource number is a byte, so a v2 DIR can hold at most 256 entries × 3 = **768 bytes**.
★★★★ **This is not a censused maximum with one slot of margin — it is the width of the index.**
Every DIR file on disk is padded to exactly 768, and the census confirms what the format already
guarantees. The assertion in `memmap.inc` is therefore checkable at assembly and does not depend on
any census staying current.

★★ **Freed: 4 × 256 = 1,024 bytes at `$1C00-$2000`, and nothing else moves.** Growing
`MAP_RESERVED` instead would mean sliding `MAP_CODE` to `$1C00`, and `p3b_run.lua` pokes `0x2000`
while `p3b_probe.s` orgs `MAP_CODE` — disturbing the integration probe for the same 1,024 bytes.

**C. The constant had FIVE homes and the fifth cost 9/9.** [authority: measurement]
`memmap.inc`, `res_core.s`, `res_sweep.lua`, `p3b_run.lua` and `vm_sweep.lua`. Changing three took
the resource gate to **348/1,264 with "empty-slot" on every PICTURE above 65**; changing four left
`vm_sweep.lua` and the **VM gate reported all nine titles diverging** — which is what a broken
interpreter looks like, from a constant in a Lua host [L-54].

> ★★★★ **Both gates did exactly their job and both named a symptom several files from the cause.**
> That is the §2F violation making itself felt, and it is **inherited, not introduced**: one number,
> five files, no check that they agree except the gates themselves.

**D. §2M decided where the decoder goes, and the answer avoids trigger 3 entirely.** [§2M]
`HAL_input_poll` is detection-only — its own header defers directional decode and it returns
`B = 0`. `input.s` is **SHARED**, and §2M.4 says an AGI-only export in a shared file is drift even
when guarded. `hal_globals.s` is **PROJECT_LOCAL for all three repos** (`hal_sync_check.py`'s own
`PROJECT_LOCAL` mapping names it), so the decoder lands in AGI alone. ★★★ **POP and Karateka are
not rebuilt byte-identical — their inputs do not change at all**, which is the stronger property.
`hal_sync_check.py` stays green in all three.

★★★★ **And it is guarded, because it broke a probe that has nothing to do with the keyboard.**
Unguarded, the 318 bytes pushed `pic_probe.s` past its own layout assertion —
*"pic_probe code overlaps PIC_DATA"*. Behind `HAL_KEYBOARD`, a build pays for what it asks for:
HAL 1,152 without, 1,470 with. ★★ **The guard name is `HAL_KEYBOARD` and not `HAL_KEY_SCAN`
because lwasm matched the latter against the label `HAL_key_scan` and reported "Multiply defined
symbol"** — a flag that collides with the symbol it guards.

**E. One figure I could not reconcile, stated rather than smoothed.** `pic_probe.bin` builds at
**2,654** where `run_gates.sh`'s header records **2,642** for the same flags. My decoder is guarded
off for that build and emits nothing, and the pic gate passes 45/45 with every per-picture hash
identical — so the binary is right and the recorded size is a **12-byte fossil**, the same class as
the cel gate's 6,782 that `run_gates.sh` itself documents. ★★ **I did not chase it to its origin**
and it is in §7.
