### 3 — Reasoning

**★★★★★ Why the first breakdown would have inverted the answer, twice over.**

*First*, `P3_T_VM..P3_T_RENDER` have existed since P3b.1, are **zeroed every cycle and never
written** — declared, cleared, dead. The host read nothing from them because there was nothing to
read. **AC-5's breakdown had no producer at all.** Replaced with the write-tap mechanism
`pic_probe` has used since T-P0-012: the guest stores a phase number, MAME stamps exact emulated
time at the store, one-instruction resolution, and the guest pays one `sta` rather than counting
cycles it would then have to subtract from itself.

*Second*, `vm_pace` is a **busy-wait** — it spins on `vm_step_clock` until `vm_passed` reaches
`vm_tdelay`. Timed as a single "vm" stage it read 0.156 s/cycle, which conflates *waiting on
purpose* with *interpreting*. ★★★ **A budget that cannot separate waiting from working cannot
answer "is it fast enough."** Split, pace is 4.3% and interpret is the cost — so the shortfall was
**not** pacing, which is what sent me to look at the clock.

**★★★★ The 2.003× ratio is what named the clock.** interpret measured 252,969 CPU cycles/VM cycle
against AD-87's 126,307. A performance difference is not a round number; **an exact factor of two
is a clock, a stride, or a width.** Rebuilt with `-DHAL_SYS_FAST_CLOCK`, every stage halved and
interpret landed within 0.16% of AD-87 — which is simultaneously the fix and the calibration.

**★★★ The watchdog manufactured a failure before any of this.** It fired at `stuck == 240` frames
— 4 emulated seconds — while its message claimed 900. The room render alone is ~2.9 s, so the
first run reported "STUCK in cycle 1" for a cycle doing its job. **A watchdog shorter than the
work it guards manufactures the failure it is looking for**, and a message that misreports its own
threshold denies the reader the one number they would judge by. Both now come from `STALL_FRAMES`.

**★★ The localiser earned its place** [L-59]. The PC census resolved the "hang" to `res_fetch`'s
word loop, `res_cache_stash`'s copy, the fill's span walk and `put_pixel` — a flat distribution
across legitimate work, which is what said *slow, not stuck*.

**Authority tier.** All measurement of our own tree; no ScummVM or Specs claim, so §2.1 does not
arise. **§2S:** POP `104b197` wip, Karateka `29f8f0a` wip.

  is one missing `-D` away from being wrong by 2×.**

  not what produced it.
