### 3 — Reasoning

**§3(2) — all six `plane_*` call sites, with loop depth.** The dispatch says seven; **there are
six**:

| line | call | enclosing label | depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| 331 | `plane_pri` | `co_opaque` | ★★★★★ **per opaque pixel** |

| 596 | `plane_vis` | `co_put_visual` | ★★★★★ **per drawn pixel** |
| 770 | `plane_pri` | `co_cc_read` | ★★ **per row of a control-line column walk** |

★★★ **Five of six are per-pixel; only `co_cc_read`'s is not.** ★★ P6.87's `co_prix` cache already
removed one re-derivation between sites 331 and 371, so the measured rate is **1.63 calls per opaque
pixel**, not the 4 the site list suggests.

**§3(3) — what `plane_pri` costs, counted from the source:**

```
pshs a 7 · lsra x5 = 10 · adda ph_blk_pri 5 · cmpa ph_cur5 5 · beq 3
pp_have: puls a 7 · anda #$1F 2 · addd pl_pri_base 6 · tfr d,x 6 · rts 5   = 56
plus the jsr at the call site                                              =  8
                                                                            ---
                                                            on a HIT        64
```

★★★★★ **Of those 64, 50 are hoistable** — the `jsr`/`rts`, the `pshs`/`puls`, the five `lsra` slice
derivation and the slice test — **and 14 must stay**: `anda`/`addd`/`tfr` is the address itself,
which is per pixel by definition. ★★ On a miss it also `bsr pl_map_pri`, and the hit rate is high
because a row of 160 bytes crosses a 8,192-byte slice about once in 51.

**§4A's arithmetic, on the CURRENT build** (composite 0.12739 s/cycle measured = 227,999 CPU cycles
per game cycle at 1.789772 MHz; 725.4 tested / 329.6 opaque / 255.9 written pixels per cycle):

- plane access **15.1%** of the stage = 34,428 cycles = **47.5 cycles per tested pixel**
- blit body **47.3%** = 107,844 cycles = **148.7 cycles per tested pixel**
- implied **538 `plane_*` calls per cycle = 1.63 per opaque pixel**
- hoisting all of them saves 50/64 of 34,428 = 26,897 cycles = **11.8% of the stage = 6.4% of a
  cycle**

★★★ **I re-profiled rather than reusing P6.97's shares**, because those predate P6.98 — which is
the trap P6.97 itself caught.

**Authority tier.** All measured in this tree at a measured clock. ★ The 64- and 50-cycle figures are
**instruction counts from the source, my own arithmetic, labelled unverified per §8** — the 47.5 that
matters is measured.

**§2H's three checks.** (1) *A second mechanism for a different object class?* ★★★★★ **Yes, and it is
this task's finding**: the cost is not one mechanism but **five per-pixel labels**, and the largest
(`co_pix`, 13.9%) is the one that runs for *transparent* pixels too — a different population from the
opaque ones the plane calls serve. (2) *The calling routine.* The plane calls are inside
`co_opaque`/`co_depth`/`co_st_put`, so their frequency is set by the **opaque** pixel count (329.6),
not the tested count (725.4) — which is why 1.63 calls/opaque pixel is the right denominator.
(3) *Grep the reports.* P6.97 (159 cyc/pixel), P6.98 (per-call overhead dominates short batches),
P6.52 (the per-access choice), P6.87 (`co_prix`) all agree — **and P6.97's "the map has globals only"
is corrected here.**

**§2S.** No sibling claim is made.
