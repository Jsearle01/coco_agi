### 1 — Summary

**Part 1: the level poll is now an edge.** `p3_key_edge` is the one scanner outside the VBL arm.
A key counts only when the scan differs from the previous scan and is non-zero, and a release
resets the remembered key. `p3_poll_dir` dispatches in the oracle's order and forwards to the
editor in every arm. `p3_poll_key` drains instead of scanning. The park latch records edges.
`tx_wait_dismiss` takes edges. §4C's four traces are identical at two cycle rates; the level fault
arm is red on each.

★★★★★ **Part 2, the extension: the cause of "Graham won't move".** The eye gate after part 1 still
showed no movement. The headless trace showed the game's own logic taking control of the ego three
steps into a walk. **The combined arm writes sprite pixels into the staged game data.**
- `p3_composite_all` fetches each sprite's VIEW with `res_open`, which maps a volume block into
  slot 6.
- `plane_win.s` caches which framebuffer slice it believes slot 6 holds, and skipped the remap.
- So `co_put_visual` stored doubled pixels (`$BB`, `$33`) into KQ1's volume 1 through the volume
  window.
- Logic 1 is re-fetched from that volume every cycle, and became corrupted bytecode. At cycle 18 it
  fell into the alligator-death block: `program.control`, `stop.motion`, `follow.ego` on both
  alligators, `print(1)`.

**One `jsr plane_reset` after the VIEW fetch fixes it.**

This explains, in one defect:
- **Graham freezing after three steps, then ignoring every key** (P6.77's x=107, and this task's
  first eye gate)
- **the alligators chasing out of the moat**, at least the chase itself (P6.73 onward); see §7.1
- **the `"` box at ~cycle 57** (P6.75 onward), which was `print(1)`, the death message

★★★★ **Found by comparing ARMS, not builds.** Given the same VAR 6 = 3 at the same park, the
**text arm matched the reference through cycle 19 and the combined arm did not**, with no key at
all. The subsystem the passing arm lacks, the compositor, was the suspect. Taps then walked it back:
flag 63's writer (logic 1), the bytes of logic 1's copy, the write into the arena (the stash loop,
carrying bad source), and finally a range tap on the draw windows filtered to volume block `$0E`,
which named `co_put_visual`.

**Jay, final eye gate:** *"graham seems to move fine but i have to hold down the arrow key for a bit
for it to resgister"*; alligators *"do not stay in the moat"*; *"no box"*; *"each leter does appear
once but typing is vry slow."* The last two are the once-per-cycle scan missing a tap that starts
and ends between looks, which only the parked 60 Hz capture can see (§7.1).

### 3 — Reasoning

**§3(1)** Nine arms verified at P6.77's figures before any change.

**§3(2) ★★★★★ Every per-cycle reader of key state, and what each became:**

| reader | was | now |
|---|---|---|
| `p3_poll_dir` (cel-linked arms) | `jsr HAL_key_scan`, **level**, once a cycle | `p3_key_event`: **edge** (pending park edge, else a scan); the dispatcher |
| `p3_poll_key` (text-prompt arms) | latched key, else **live level scan** | **drains `p3_keybuf`**; in text-only arms, which have no `p3_poll_dir`, it takes the one edge itself, every cycle, and drops it if the prompt is off |

| VBL arm (`-DP3B_VBLKEYS_OPT`) | edge queue | **unchanged, byte-identical** (18866 / B38C1357 before and after) |
| `input_probe.s` | its own debounce | a separate probe, untouched |

★★★ **§1.2's premise, "two scanners are themselves the problem", CONFIRMED, and it was three in the
combined arm** (dispatcher, `p3_poll_key`'s fallback, and the park latch, plus the box wait). All
were level readers, so a held letter could reach the editor once per cycle. **Now one edge
detector, one pending slot, one dispatcher.**

**§3(3)** `P3B_HOLDKEY` used as the proof. Extended: letter field names contain spaces (`k  K`,
per keymatrix_probe), and the first pattern only accepted alphanumerics.

**§3(4) `tx_wait_dismiss` with a held ENTER:** it dismissed on the level, so a box opened while
ENTER was still down closed at once. Shown by §4C(4)'s fault arm.

#### §4A — the oracle (pin `9d9b9e93`)
1. ★★★★ **Letters and ENTER DO repeat in the oracle, and the rate is ScummVM's, not AGI's.**
   `keyboard.cpp:208` (`if ((key) && (key <= 0xFF))`) accepts them with **no `kbdRepeat` test**.
   Only the direction keys test it (`:226-262`, `if (_allowSynthetic || !event.kbdRepeat)`). The
   repeats come from `common/events.cpp:222-294`, `KeyboardRepeatEventSourceWrapper`:
   `kKeyRepeatInitialDelay = 400, kKeyRepeatSustainDelay = 100`. **Platform policy.** A port with no
   OS would have to invent one, and a ~4 Hz poll could not deliver 100 ms repeats. **Edges only;
   the gap is named** in `p3_key_edge`'s header.
2. **"Same direction again" STOPS; it does not toggle or reverse**: `if (screenObjEgo->direction ==
   newDirection) setVar(VM_VAR_EGO_DIRECTION, 0); else setVar(..., newDirection)`
   [`keyboard.cpp:603-607`]. A third press sets it again.

### 7 — Uncertainty flags

#### 7.1 ★★★★ For Jay — what is left, and why
- **A quick tap is missed.** The port looks at the keyboard once per cycle (~4 a second) and in the
  short park between cycles. A tap that starts and ends between two looks is never seen, so you have
  to hold. **Typing is slow for the same reason.** Edges made each press count once; they cannot see
  a press that happened entirely between looks. **The fix is the 60 Hz capture that was parked in
  P6.75** — and ★★★★ **its failure then ("everything animating, nothing moving… the alligators
  stopped") matches this task's corruption closely**: the death branch takes control of Graham and
  stops everything. **The latch may have been failing on THIS defect, not on bounce.** Worth
  re-testing now.
- **The alligators still leave the moat.** The chase that `follow.ego` started is gone, but they
  still wander out. The port omits `checkPriority`/`checkCollision` (`vm_objects.s:109, 490`), the
  engine routines that keep an object on its allowed water or land. Nothing holds a wandering
  alligator in the moat.

#### 7.2 Other flags
- ★★★ **Every p3b run before this fix had corrupted staged game data** from the first composited
  sprite. Any headless finding in the castle after ~cycle 8 since the alligators first drew (P6.74)
  was taken on a corrupted logic 1. **Named for re-reading, not re-deriving**: P6.75's latch eye
  gate, P6.77's typing-after-the-box position (the box itself was the corruption's), and the x=107
  freeze.
- **The logic cache does not survive a cycle in the combined arm.** Logic 0 and 1 are re-fetched
  every cycle (measured by the `$77B1` tap). Probably the compositor's depth-0 VIEW fetches
  evicting it when the arena is full. **A performance finding, not pursued**: re-fetching every
  cycle is L-66 again.
- **Staging has no integrity check in the combined arm.** A resident checksum there, the `-ResCheck`
  idea in the configuration that has a compositor, would have caught part 2 on its first cycle.
- **Headless `P3B_INJECT` typing cannot produce a response box in KQ1** without a vocabulary.
