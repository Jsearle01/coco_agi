### 3 — Reasoning

**★★★★★ Why the table padding existed, and why removing it is not a behaviour change.**
`VMTEST_TAB` held 256 two-byte entries so `vm_core.s` could take the opcode byte, double it, and
index without a bounds test. Entries 20-255 were `vm_test_unimpl` — 472 bytes whose only function
was to make an out-of-range opcode land somewhere safe. The replacement does the same job in 9
bytes: an opcode ≥ `VMTEST_MAX` now reaches `vm_test_unimpl` through a branch instead of through
padding. **Same handler, same halt, same reported opcode.** ★★ The check loads `X` and **falls
into the same `jsr ,x`** rather than branching past it, so `vm_exitall`, `vm_skip_instruction` and
the NOT/OR handling run identically — jumping around the dispatch would have skipped them.
★ `bhs`, not `bge`: `vm_op` is a byte and opcodes above `$7F` must compare as large, not negative.

**★★★ Three measurement methods, two of them wrong, and the tool records which.** Counting the
listing's hex column gave 12,577 against 13,237 actually consumed — **lwasm truncates the hex
display at 8 bytes**, so every 16-operand `fcb` row lost half its bytes, and the 660-byte
difference looked like `rmb` reservations. Spans between module starts broke on **nested
includes**: `pic_core.s` wraps `pic_draw.s` and `pic_fill.s`, so its own code is split around them
and the span read 145 against a real 588. **Address delta has neither failure** and reconciles
with the assembler's counter. ★★ Reported because the first two answers were plausible and wrong,
and a decomposition nobody can check is worth as little as the comment it replaces [L-71].

**★★★★ Why only the VM gate needed re-running (L-71, and it saved four gate runs).** The change
is inside `vm_tables.s`, which only `vm_probe.s`, `p3b_probe.s`, `p3b_boot_test.s` and
`vm_state.s` include. `gate_audit.py --verify` shows **pic, res, cel and comp rebuild
byte-identical** after the change (2,642 / 2,019 / 1,436 / 967) and **vm differs** (9,499 →
9,036). **Byte-identity of the artifact is a stronger claim than re-running the gate**, and it is
the claim an mtime could not make. The VM gate was re-run because its artifact moved.

**★★★ What fails without the bytes** [L-64, asked of my own change rather than a cut]. Nothing.
The 236 removed table entries are unreachable by construction from valid v2 bytecode, and
unreachable-with-the-same-outcome from invalid bytecode. **This is not a divergence with a cost;
it is padding with no function.** ★★ Contrast with a reservation cut, where what fails is a
parser nobody has written yet — which is precisely why the ruling was to fix the memory instead.

**Authority tier.** Measurement of our own tree and the generated tables' own source
(`optable.py`, itself generated from the pinned oracle). No ScummVM behavioural claim is at issue.
